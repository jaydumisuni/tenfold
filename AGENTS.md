## Non-negotiable identity

Tenfold is a **model-free machine execution force for completing an already-approved engineering roadmap at maximum safe parallelism**.

Tenfold is not the architect.

Sergeant is the primary organisational donor. Preserve the donor principle:

> **Training is shared. Authority is not.**

Do not replace that hierarchy with a generic autonomous-agent swarm.

## Command boundary

The conceptual chain is:

```text
Approved Blueprint
  -> deterministic Foreman
  -> Execution Officers
  -> trained bounded Privates
  -> Facilities
  -> structured evidence
  -> Milestone Council
  -> mandatory external assurance where Matrix requires
  -> next safe frontier
```

Consultant models are optional advisory capabilities. They are never required for normal execution and never mutate campaign authority directly.

## Private boundary

A Private executes its bounded job. It does not redesign the blueprint, alter the DAG, expand scope, spawn authoritative work, issue final verdicts, waive coupling rules, or decide architecture.

Unexpected evidence goes to the responsible Officer under the task's stop/escalation contract.

## Foreman boundary

The Foreman is deterministic/model-free by default. It computes from frozen authority; it does not exercise open-ended architectural discretion.

Mandatory external assurance comes from the exact bound Assurance Matrix, not Foreman preference.

## Dependency-frontier law

Do not serialize roadmap phases merely because they are numbered.

Continuously occupy the full useful safe dependency frontier.

Known dependencies must cause one of:

- safe independent execution;
- exact frozen-contract binding;
- preparation-safe work;
- blocking/serialization.

Never guess merely to keep workers busy.

## Mutation law

Abundant labour does not mean abundant writers.

> **Parallelize reasoning aggressively; parallelize mutation only where independence is proven or safely bounded.**

For high-risk mutable work, unknown independence defaults to serialization.

## Council law

Do not equate completion with correctness.

Programme/milestone boundaries require Council reconciliation. External assurance is added deterministically where the Matrix requires it.
